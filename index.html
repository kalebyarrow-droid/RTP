Failure Management Model

Initial stage

I HAVE CONTROL- Fly the aircraft

  • PFD (FPV relative to horizon)
  • Trim for pitch/Rudder
  • Call for appropriate modes
  • Engage AP/AT
  • MSA/SEFP

“AIRCRAFT IS SAFE AND IN CONTROL - CONFIRM THE FAILURE”

Immediate recall items

  • CON thrust if necessary
  • APU
  • CB (can’t fix a system if the CB has popped)

Checklist sequence

  • NNC
  • System recall
  • NNC
  • NC
  • DDG

Constantly review a Short term plan/ATC (MAYDAY/PAN PAN/ATC/CTAF)

Make a plan: Continue, Return or Divert (FORDECR)

F

Facts (gather the facts)

  • Root PROBLEM
  • What can we still do (fuel, IAP, systems)?
  • Biggest priority?
O

Options

  • How much FUEL do we have
  • What does that allow us to do (Continue, Divert, Hold)
  • Available airports (Wx/Conditions, Enroute)
R

Risks/Benefits

  • What are the RISKS and BENEFITS of each previous OPTION?
D

Decide

  • Decide on the safest option
  • Crew input
E

Execute

  • Execute the plan
C

Communicate (FACPO then SBA)

  • FO: Ensure they understand the plan and satisfied, do they have anything to add?
  • ATC
  • CM: Brief CM, CM to brief crew, Captain to make PA
  • Pax: PA
  • Operations:
  • Setup (x5 B’s: Box, Baros, Bearings and Pointers, Bugs, Brakes)
  • Brief
  • Additional checklists
R

Review

  • Is the plan still working?
  • Is there new information?

Missed Approach

In the event of a missed approach, return to the beginning of FORDEC.

Return to gate

Stabilise and confirm

  • Fly the aircraft - STOP and park the brake
  • Short term plan
  • Advise ATC
  • Confirm the failure
  • Memory items
  • CB’s

NNC and dispatch

1. Complete any NNC:

  • - If NNC says either ‘Do not take off’ or ‘LANSA’ the flight shall RTG

2. Consult the DDG:

  • - Not listed in the DDG, or prohibits dispatch for the type of operation (eg EDTO) then RTG
  • - If (M) is listed the procedure must be completed prior to takeoff, thus RTG
  • - If (O) is listed the PIC must be satisfied any flight considerations can be complied with.
  • - After dispatch the MEL must be applied at the place of landing.

3. Contact Maintenance Watch.

Pretend you have just landed, do everything in the same order

If returning to gate

  • FO: After landing scan
  • Contact AMCO, inform of situation and request gate
  • Start the APU if not already
  • Inform CS
  • PA to pax

Additional reminders

  • ATC Fly the aircraft - STOP and park the brake
  • Short term plan (emergency PA’s?)
  • Advise ATC you have stopped
